## Scope Confirmation

Before starting, confirm:

- The audit is based on public page evidence unless otherwise agreed.
- Findings are likely conversion risks and improvement opportunities, not guaranteed revenue outcomes.
- Recommendations should be reviewed and tested against the client's analytics, margins, product constraints, and brand requirements.
- Any implementation work is separate from the audit unless explicitly included.
